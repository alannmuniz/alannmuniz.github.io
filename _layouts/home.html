Research interests

I am a researcher in Algebraic and Complex geometries focusing on distributions and foliations, moduli of (parabolic) sheaves, and tensors. Some of my topics of interest are the following:

  • Automorphism groups of holomorphic distributions and foliations;
  • Parabolic vector bundles over Riemann surfaces and moduli spaces of logarithmic connections;
  • Moduli spaces of holomorphic distributions and foliations;
  • Moduli spaces of sheaves and related topics;
  • Varieties of tensors and Euclidean distance degree.
