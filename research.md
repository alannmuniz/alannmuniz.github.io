## Research interests

My research focuses on distributions and foliations, moduli of sheaves and parabolic bundles, and the geometry of tensors. Current themes include:

- automorphism groups of holomorphic distributions and foliations;
- moduli spaces of holomorphic distributions and foliations;
- parabolic vector bundles over Riemann surfaces;
- moduli spaces of logarithmic connections;
- moduli spaces of sheaves and related constructions;
- varieties of tensors and Euclidean distance degree.

## Selected themes

### Foliations and distributions

I study geometric and moduli-theoretic questions for holomorphic foliations and distributions, including their singular schemes, symmetry groups, and classification problems.

### Sheaves and vector bundles

A second line concerns rank-two bundles and reflexive sheaves on projective varieties, together with monadic descriptions and moduli spaces.

### Parabolic and logarithmic geometry

This direction includes parabolic vector bundles on curves and moduli spaces of logarithmic connections.

### Tensor geometry

I am also interested in projective varieties arising from tensors and in Euclidean distance degree as an invariant of algebraic optimization problems.
